## Overview

NestShopify sells directly to customers, from browsing through payment and delivery. The backend follows the Controller, Service, Repository pattern.

## Key features

- Registration, email verification, Google login and ACL permissions
- Role and permission management
- Category and item management with automatic stock updates after purchase
- Vouchers limited by time and quantity
- Flash sales with automatic email reminders 15 minutes before start
- Monthly and yearly sales reports
